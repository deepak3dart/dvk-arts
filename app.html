Workflow

Six steps from blank page
to a growing body of work.

01

Snap a reference

Photograph or import any reference image directly into the app.

02

Set your target

Define a sketch goal — how many studies, by when, and which category.

03

Draw your sketch

Work at your own pace, on paper or digital. Sketchlog stays out of your way.

04

Mark it done

Log the completed sketch in seconds. Add a photo of your work.

05

Watch progress

See streaks, category breakdowns, and weekly charts build over time.

06

Export your portfolio

Generate a clean PDF of any date range to share or archive.
