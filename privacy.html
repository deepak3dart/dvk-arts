SketchLog

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

SketchLog does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. The app is designed to work entirely offline and does not require an internet connection to function.

No data collection

SketchLog has no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising integration. We do not gather any personal information from you, whether you use the app for a day or for years.

Camera access

Camera access is used solely to capture reference photos and sketches within the app. Images are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere — they are used only to display and align your photos inside SketchLog.

Local storage

Everything you create in SketchLog — reference photos, sketches, categories, targets, progress records, and color preferences — is saved locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to any server or remote copy.

Data control & deletion

You can delete individual items from within the app at any time. Uninstalling SketchLog removes all of its data from your device. Because SketchLog has no cloud infrastructure, there are no remote copies of your data to delete separately.

Children's privacy

SketchLog does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, which makes it broadly safe to use regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If SketchLog ever introduces a feature that changes how data is handled — for example, an optional cloud backup — this policy will be updated and the date above will reflect the most recent revision.
